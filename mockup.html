Product Overview

Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a 39-amino-acid synthetic peptide functioning as a triple agonist of the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. It is supplied as a lyophilized white powder for in vitro reconstitution and is widely used as a research tool for studying receptor pharmacology, multi-incretin signaling, and metabolic pathway regulation in preclinical models.

Specifications

  • Quantity: 10 mg
  • Form: Lyophilized powder
  • CAS: 2381089-83-2
  • Molecular Weight: 4,731.33 g/mol
  • Purity: ≥99.5% (third-party verified, HPLC)
  • Endotoxin: < 1.0 EU/mg (LAL)
  • Mass deviation: ≤ 0.5 Da (MS confirmed)
  • Storage: -20°C long-term; 4°C short-term (use within 4 weeks of reconstitution)

Research Context

Retatrutide binds with high affinity to the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors — a unique triple-agonist profile that activates adenylyl cyclase and downstream cAMP/PKA signaling cascades across multiple metabolic pathways. Research involving retatrutide has examined coordinated incretin signaling, hepatic glucose output suppression, and lipolytic activity in preclinical in vitro models.
