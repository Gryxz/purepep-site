# Shipping Policy

PurePep ships research compounds with cold-chain handling, signature confirmation, and lot-matched documentation. This page explains how shipping works and what to expect.

## Where we ship

- **Domestic:** All 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia.
- **International:** Currently unavailable. International researchers should contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for institutional arrangements.

Some products are not available in certain jurisdictions and may be removed from your cart automatically based on the shipping address.

## Carriers and methods

- **Standard (2-day):** UPS 2nd Day Air or FedEx 2Day, fully insured, signature required.
- **Express (overnight):** UPS Next Day Air or FedEx Priority Overnight, fully insured, signature required.
- **Cold-chain shipments** include insulated packaging and a temperature monitor. Tracking and the monitor log are included with the shipment confirmation email.

The method available at checkout depends on the product, the destination, and current routing conditions.

## Processing time

Orders placed before **{{CUTOFF_TIME}} {{SUPPORT_TZ}}** on a business day are processed the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, or on weekends and holidays, are processed the next business day. Researcher-verification holds add up to one additional business day.

## Costs

Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on weight, destination, and service level. We do not mark up carrier rates beyond a fixed handling fee that covers cold-chain packaging and temperature monitoring.

## Tracking

A tracking number is sent by email when the carrier scans the package. You can view tracking and order status from your account at {{SITE_URL}}/account.

## Delivery and signature

All shipments require an adult signature. Plan to be at the delivery address on the delivery date. If no one is available, the carrier will attempt redelivery per its policy. Packages that are returned to us as undeliverable will be restocked; reshipment will be at your expense.

## Inspect on arrival

Open the box on the day it arrives and verify:

1. Outer box and contents are undamaged.
2. The temperature monitor (if included) shows an in-range result.
3. Lot numbers on the vials match the certificate of analysis included.

Report any issue within **72 hours of delivery** to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with photographs and the order number. See the Refund Policy for what is covered.

## Storage after delivery

Once accepted by the recipient, storage is the recipient's responsibility. Recommended storage conditions are printed on every vial label and included on the certificate of analysis.

## Lost packages

If tracking has not updated for **five business days** after the most recent scan, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} so we can open a carrier claim.

## Customs (international research institutions)

Where international shipment is arranged on a case-by-case basis, the recipient is the importer of record and is responsible for duties, taxes, and compliance with local controlled-substance and research-import regulations.
