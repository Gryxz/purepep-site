# Privacy Policy

{{LEGAL_ENTITY}} d/b/a PurePep ("PurePep", "we", "us") operates {{SITE_URL}}. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

**You give us:**

- Account and contact information — name, email, phone, shipping and billing addresses.
- Researcher-verification information — institutional email, statement of research purpose, and any documents you submit.
- Order information — items purchased, prices, dates, and the messages you send to customer service.
- Payment information — card number, expiration, CVV, and billing address. Card data is sent directly to our PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor; we receive only a tokenized reference and the last four digits.

**We collect automatically:**

- Device and browser information — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL.
- Usage information — pages visited, time on site, clickstream, and search terms within the site.
- Cookies and similar technologies — see the Cookie Notice.

**From third parties:**

- Fraud-scoring and address-verification signals from our payment processor and fraud-prevention vendors.
- Carrier delivery status from shipping partners.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. The Site is not intended for minors.

## 2. How we use information

- Fulfill orders, process payments, and arrange shipping.
- Verify researcher eligibility, age, and identity for compliance.
- Provide customer service and respond to inquiries.
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and chargebacks.
- Operate, secure, and improve the Site.
- Send transactional messages (order confirmations, shipping updates, account notices).
- Send marketing messages where permitted, with an opt-out in every email.
- Comply with law, legal process, and our Terms of Sale.

## 3. Legal bases (EU/UK/EEA residents)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: (a) **performance of a contract** to fulfill orders, (b) **legitimate interests** to prevent fraud and improve the Site, (c) **consent** for marketing where required and for non-essential cookies, and (d) **legal obligation** for tax, accounting, and compliance records.

## 4. How we share information

We share information only as described below. We do not sell personal information.

- **Service providers** — payment processing, fraud prevention, hosting, email delivery, customer-service tooling, shipping carriers, and analytics. They are bound by contract to use the information only to provide their service.
- **Compliance** — to satisfy law, court order, subpoena, or government request, or to enforce our Terms of Sale.
- **Business transfers** — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may transfer subject to standard confidentiality protections.

## 5. Payments

Card data is processed by our PCI-DSS-Level-1 compliant processor. We never see, store, or log full card numbers. Tokenized references and the last four digits are retained for fraud prevention, chargeback defense, and your order history.

## 6. Retention

We retain account, order, and tax records for as long as required by law (typically seven years for tax) and for as long as needed for fraud prevention and chargeback defense. Researcher-verification records are retained for the life of the account plus seven years. You can request deletion as described in Section 8.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS site-wide, encrypted storage, scoped access controls, audit logging, and regular security review. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your password safe.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete information (subject to legal-retention obligations).
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Receive a portable copy of your information.
- Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.

To exercise a right, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} from the address on file. We will verify identity before acting and will respond within the time required by law.

**California residents:** Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the rights above plus the right to know what categories we collect, the right to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising any right. We do not sell personal information.

**Do Not Track:** We do not currently respond to DNT signals because no common standard has been adopted.

## 9. International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or other approved mechanisms for cross-border transfers.

## 10. Children

The Site and the products sold on it are not intended for anyone under 21. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "updated" date at the top reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced on the Site at least thirty days before they take effect.
