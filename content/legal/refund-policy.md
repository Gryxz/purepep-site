# Refund Policy

**All sales final. No refunds, no exchanges, no returns.**

PurePep sells reference compounds intended exclusively for laboratory research. Because chain-of-custody, temperature control, and lot integrity cannot be verified once a product leaves our facility, we cannot accept returns or issue refunds after shipment. This policy is presented at checkout, in our Terms of Sale, and on every product page.

## The two exceptions

The only circumstances in which we will reship or issue store credit are:

### 1. Damage in transit

If a shipment arrives visibly damaged, with broken vials, or with a confirmed temperature-monitor breach, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within **72 hours of delivery** with:

- Order number
- Photographs of the outer box and the affected product
- The temperature-monitor reading if included
- Description of the issue

On verification we will, at our option, ship a replacement of the affected items or issue store credit equal to their value.

### 2. Carrier loss

If tracking confirms a shipment was lost in transit, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the order number. We will open a carrier claim. If the carrier confirms the loss in writing, we will, at our option, ship a replacement or issue store credit.

## What is not a basis for refund

- Buyer's remorse, change of plans, or accidental order.
- Researcher decided not to use the product.
- Delivery delay caused by the carrier.
- Customs delay, refusal, or seizure outside the U.S.
- Address entered incorrectly at checkout.
- Refusal to accept delivery.
- Storage failure after the package was accepted by the recipient.

## Chargebacks

If you have a problem with an order, contact us first. Initiating a chargeback before contacting customer service is a breach of our Terms of Sale. We retain shipment records, temperature data, signed delivery confirmations, and access logs for every order and will present them in response to any chargeback.

## How long claims take

Damage and loss claims are acknowledged within one business day and resolved within seven business days of receiving the required documentation.
