# Cookie Notice

This Site uses cookies and similar technologies. This notice describes what they are and how you can control them.

## What cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** Session, authentication, cart persistence, fraud-prevention, and CSRF tokens. The Site does not work without these. They cannot be disabled through our consent banner.

**Functional.** Remembered preferences such as researcher-verification state and locale.

**Analytics.** Aggregated traffic and product-performance data. We use these to improve the catalog and checkout. No card data or government identifiers are passed to analytics.

**Advertising.** None. We do not run third-party advertising, retargeting pixels, or audience-building tags on the Site.

## Choices

You can refuse non-essential cookies through the consent banner shown on your first visit and reopened from the link in the footer. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser; this may break parts of the Site such as cart and checkout.

## Do Not Track

We do not currently respond to DNT signals because no common standard has been adopted.
