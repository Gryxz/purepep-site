## What to include when you write

To help us answer quickly:

1. Your order number (if applicable).
2. The email address on the account.
3. The product, lot, or COA number you are asking about.
4. A clear description of the issue, with photographs for any damage report.

## What we cannot help with

- Dosing, administration, or therapeutic questions. PurePep does not provide medical guidance, and our products are not for human use.
- Re-creating tracking that has been delivered. After the carrier confirms delivery, retention of the package becomes the recipient's responsibility.
- Refunds outside the limited exceptions in the Refund Policy.

## Chargebacks

If you are unhappy with an order, contact us before initiating a chargeback. We resolve damage and loss claims within seven business days of receiving the required documentation. Initiating a chargeback before contacting us is a breach of our Terms of Sale.
