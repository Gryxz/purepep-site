# Frequently asked questions

## Who can buy from PurePep?

Qualified researchers who are at least 21 years old and intend to use the product for in-vitro laboratory work. **Sales restricted to qualified researchers, 21 and over.** We may request institutional email or a statement of research purpose before fulfilling an order.

## Are these products for human use?

No. **For research use only. Not for human consumption.** Products are not drugs, supplements, or cosmetics and are not approved by the FDA for any therapeutic, diagnostic, or preventative use.

## What if my shipment arrives damaged?

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within **72 hours of delivery** with the order number and photographs. We will reship or issue store credit on verification. See the Refund Policy.

## What if my package is lost?

If tracking has not updated for five business days, email us. We open a carrier claim and, on confirmed loss, reship or issue store credit.

## Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

No. **All sales final. No refunds, no exchanges, no returns.** This is required by chain-of-custody and lot-integrity standards.

## Do you provide a certificate of analysis?

Yes — every shipment includes a lot-matched COA with HPLC purity, identity confirmation, and the manufacturer's release data. Past COAs are downloadable from your account.

## How are the products stored before shipping?

Lyophilized peptides are stored cold and shipped in insulated packaging with a temperature monitor. The monitor reading is included with the shipment confirmation.

## How do I store the product after delivery?

Storage instructions are printed on every vial label and listed on the COA. Once accepted by the recipient, storage is the recipient's responsibility.
