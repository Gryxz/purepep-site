# About PurePep

PurePep is a U.S. supplier of research-grade peptides operated by **{{LEGAL_ENTITY}}**. We sell reference compounds to qualified researchers for in-vitro laboratory work. We do not sell to consumers, and our products are not therapeutics.

## What we do

- Source peptides from manufacturers that publish full synthesis records.
- Run **triplicate HPLC** identity and purity assays on every lot.
- Issue a **lot-matched certificate of analysis** with every shipment.
- Ship cold-chain with temperature monitoring, signature required, fully insured.
- Maintain documentation on file for every vial that leaves the building.

## What we will not do

- Sell to anyone who cannot confirm they are 21 or over and engaged in research.
- Make medical, therapeutic, or dietary claims about any product.
- Provide dosing guidance, prescribe, or counsel on human use.
- Ship to addresses we cannot verify or to jurisdictions where a product is restricted.

## Compliance

- **For research use only. Not for human consumption.**
- **Sales restricted to qualified researchers, 21 and over.**
- **All sales final. No refunds, no exchanges, no returns.**

These statements appear on every product page, in cart, at checkout, and in our Terms of Sale.
